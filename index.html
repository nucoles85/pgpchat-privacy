pgpchat Privacy Policy

App Store name: pgpchat-tor · Effective September 30, 2026

In short: pgpchat collects no data. There are no accounts, no phone numbers, no analytics and no advertising, and no one — including the developer — runs a server that can read or store your messages.

What pgpchat collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information, usage data, contacts, messages, identifiers or location. The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code and makes no connections to the developer.

How messages travel

  • Messages go directly from your device to your contact's device over the Tor network, which hides both devices' IP addresses.
  • Every message is end-to-end encrypted, with a new key for each message that is destroyed after use (forward secrecy). Only you and your contact can read it.
  • If a contact is offline, a message may be held by a relay: your own relay, or another of your contacts who has agreed to relay. A relay holds only encrypted data it cannot read, keeps it in memory, and deletes it once delivered. It learns which contact's address a message is for, but never its content.

What stays on your device

Your encryption keys, contacts, invites and (only if you turn on disappearing messages with a timer) message history are stored on your device, encrypted, and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. By default, messages are not saved at all and disappear when the app closes. Wipe everything in Settings erases all of it immediately.

Device permissions

  • Camera — only to scan invite and verification QR codes. Images are processed on the device and never saved or sent.
  • Face ID / passcode — only to unlock the app if you turn on App Lock. The check happens in iOS; pgpchat never receives biometric data.

Apple and TestFlight

If you install pgpchat through Apple's App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect information under Apple's privacy policy — for example your TestFlight invitation email, and crash reports or feedback if you choose to send them. Crash reports and feedback reach the developer only through Apple and contain no message content.

Children

pgpchat is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
